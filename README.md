## Case Studies

### Scout - AI Operator Inside a Working Business
CW Real Estate | Sep 2026

An AI operator that works the way a staff member works: it reads the live systems, makes the change, writes down what it changed, and reports. Not a chatbot bolted onto a product.

It runs the company console at leads.cwlagos.com - one front door used daily by employees, agents and admins. Agents, support, heads, directors and owner each see only their own work. Leads, contacts, deals and clients stay in sync with the CRM. Follow-ups carry a one-hour clock. Instagram conversations become support tickets and then CRM leads in one click. The Academy (327 registrations) runs from registration through certificates. Nightly full CRM export (16,264 records across 15 modules on 23 Sep, zero failures) plus a morning integrity check.

Three operating habits: Sweep first, ask second. Do everything that does not need a person. Write down every change.

The interesting engineering is the failure handling that came from real incidents (shared tokens, safe retries, body-based write checks, safety caps on paginated reads, backup pointers that only move on complete nights).

Live and in daily use. The operator and the record of what it did are the asset. The pattern is portable.

### CW Real Estate - CRM & Operating System
CRM Automation Architect & Zoho One Administrator | 2025-Present

The CRM had become admin, not an asset. 1,098 leads sat with no status. Hundreds of property records lived in Airtable and never made it cleanly across. Logging a lead meant wading through more than a hundred fields, so agents often simply did not.

What changed:
- Recovered 1,098 stalled leads into a reportable pipeline
- Migrated and de-duplicated 683 property records with automatic ownership mapping
- Replaced the 100+ field screen with a phone-first console that asks for three things
- Website enquiry becomes a matched, deduplicated, assigned lead with best-fit properties in about two minutes
- Self-healing data hygiene and multi-party deal model enforced by automation
- Offer letters and invoices moved to a secure web app with central numbering and approval

One clean system the team actually opens, on the phones they already carry. Data stays trustworthy without adding ops headcount.

### CW Real Estate - Multi-Agent AI Systems

Designed and operate a production multi-agent system (OpenClaw + Claude) that owns parts of the operation.

Specialised agents share persistent memory and run work autonomously instead of reactively. A scheduled enquiry engine ingests website enquiries, matches them to live properties, creates the Zoho lead and pushes the contact into nurture - about two minutes end to end, no human in the loop. Agents also keep Airtable and the CRM in step. The conversational assistant was moved off a laptop onto the cloud so it answers from live CRM data around the clock.

Operational work scales with systems, not headcount.

### INTLAB (Italy) - International Multilingual CRM
Director of CRM & Automation Infrastructure | Jan-Mar 2026 (remote)

A social app preparing to launch needed to capture and convert early registrations at scale across several audiences and languages. Ad spend was already going out with no organised pipeline.

Built the Zoho One backbone from zero with dedicated modules for affiliates, creators and fans. WATI chatbots in five languages (English, French, Portuguese, Spanish, Italian) qualified and converted registrants in their own language. Meta Ads Instant Forms fed leads straight into the CRM in real time.

3,000+ early registrations converted in a three-month window. Ad spend turned into a structured, contactable pipeline instead of a list nobody follows up.
